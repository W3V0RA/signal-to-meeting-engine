# Business readiness and acceptance

There are two separate states: the software is implemented/tested for local installation; a particular business is verified for a future authorized live scope. The first does not imply the second.

## Send-disabled installation acceptance

- Node 24+ and the included checks pass on the target machine.
- Setup preserves existing files and creates separate private credentials, config and durable state.
- The loopback operator console authenticates, displays exact copy and refuses stale reviews/edits.
- The application executes durable scheduled work and survives restart without duplicate digests or transport.
- Source/permission/suppression/history and uncertainty remain visible; every outbound channel stays held.
- A consistent backup and restoration into a separate held database are reviewed.

## Business live-readiness evidence

- Accurate business identity, real signature/address, relevant offers, targets and reviewed permission.
- Business-specific source captures, exact person/role and published route, honest reviewed copy.
- Actual sender account/alias and Reply-To; fresh successful inbound reads and enabled Calendar/Notion mapping checks.
- Six existing selected owned-inbox artifacts proving aligned received authentication, TLS, exact initial copy, mirrored reply route and actual unsubscribe, plus local exact-route suppression enforcement.
- Current authenticated sender/integration proof bound to the business config. Simulation and manually pasted passing booleans cannot clear it.
- Separate explicit human activation, followed by current individual approval and expiring exact-purpose message release, under caps/window/spacing and suppression/history checks.

`npm run doctor` intentionally reports pending business verification in the shipped example. Installation, the UI and verification commands cannot activate sending. No business account, live receipt, deployment, digest delivery, booking or revenue has been fabricated for this repository.

The proof collector currently verifies a controlled Gmail-to-owned-Gmail route from actual API reads. This is limited route evidence, not a guarantee for all providers or future messages. Proof and activation expire within one hour; a future operating window needs genuinely current artifacts. Actual app-to-recipient transport, owner receipt and target-specific behavior still require a separately authorized owned-inbox rollout after passive readiness checks. Never test on prospects or infer permission from a technical pass.

Public hosting, multi-tenant access, distributed workers, autonomous model writing and unrestricted semantic claim checking require separate design/verification and are not part of this local installation contract.
